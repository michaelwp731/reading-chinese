Nihao Reading Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Nihao Reading (“the app”, “we”) is an app for reading Chinese. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, who else receives it, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

1. What we collect and why

Account information. An account identifier, and your email address and name if you choose to sign in. To keep your data tied to you, to sync it across your devices, and to apply usage limits. Our authentication and database service providers

Product usage data is collected only in anonymous form and is not combined with data from other companies. Reading activity is linked to your account but is used only by us to personalize the app.

2. Who receives your data

Our app uses third-party service providers for specific functions, such as cloud hosting, account authentication and data storage, text recognition, translation, speech generation, subscription management, and product analytics. When you use these features, the data they need is sent to those providers, including third-party AI services, solely to deliver the feature to you. We select providers carefully and require them to protect your data, but we recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these services for complete information on their practices.

Payments are processed by Apple; we never see your payment card details. We may also disclose data if required by law. We do not sell or rent personal information.

3. Device permissions

  • Camera — only when you choose to scan a book page. The photo is sent for text recognition and kept for up to 7 days for troubleshooting, then deleted.
  • Files / Photos — only the PDF or image you explicitly select is read.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Account and learning data are kept until you delete your account. Data tied to an anonymous ID (no account) is deleted after about 60 days without use. Signing out removes this data from your device; it stays in your account.
  • Reading activity is kept for up to 180 days (30 days for which stories were shown to you). It is deleted when you turn off Me → Personalized recommendations or delete your account.
  • Feedback you send is deleted when you delete your account. Screenshots attached to feedback are kept for up to 180 days.
  • Photos and PDFs you import are kept for up to 7 days for troubleshooting, then deleted.
  • Books you import (the recognized text) are kept in your account until you delete the book or your account. Text you send to be read aloud is not retained by us.
  • Anonymous product usage data is kept for a limited period for analysis and is not linked to your account, so deleting your account does not remove it. You can stop further collection at any time in Me → Settings.
  • Delete your account in the app under Me → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and the learning data, imported books, reading activity, and feedback messages stored on our servers. It does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that in your Apple ID settings. Data on your device is removed by deleting the app.
  • If you cannot use the in-app option, email us (see Contact) and we will delete your data.

5. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and each account can access only its own synced data. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. International transfers

Our service providers operate servers in several countries, including the United States. By using the app you understand that your data may be processed outside your country of residence.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under GDPR or California under CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can delete your data in the app as described above, or contact us to exercise any of these rights. We do not sell personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising. You can also switch off the collection of anonymous product usage data at any time in Me → Settings → Share anonymous usage data, and turn off personalized recommendations in Me → Personalized recommendations.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
