Zhujian Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Zhujian (“the app”, “we”) is an app for reading Chinese. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, who else receives it, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

1. What we collect and why

DataWhyWhere it lives
Account information. An account identifier, and your email address and name if you choose to sign in. To keep your data tied to you, to sync it across your devices, and to apply usage limits. Our authentication and database service providers
Learning data: your learning progress and in-app preferences. To sync your progress between devices and restore it if you reinstall. On your device, and in our cloud database
Text you import (pasted text, text extracted from a PDF, or text recognized from a photo). To provide the import and reading features you request. We do not keep a copy of it. Imported books stay on your device only
Photos you take or choose for text recognition. To provide text recognition. The photo is not kept after processing. Not stored
Text you send to be read aloud. To provide narration. We do not keep a copy of it. Not stored
Purchase status (whether you have an active subscription). To unlock paid features and restore purchases. Our subscription management provider; our cloud database
Technical and usage records: account ID, request time, which feature was used, and counts toward your usage limits. These records do not contain the text, photos, or audio you process. To enforce fair-use limits, prevent abuse, and fix errors. Our cloud hosting logs, kept for a limited period

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, health data, or advertising identifier, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. Who receives your data

Our app uses third-party service providers for specific functions, such as cloud hosting, account authentication and data storage, text recognition, translation, speech generation, and subscription management. When you use these features, the data they need is sent to those providers, including third-party AI services, solely to deliver the feature to you. We select providers carefully and require them to protect your data, but we recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these services for complete information on their practices.

Payments are processed by Apple; we never see your payment card details. We may also disclose data if required by law. We do not sell or rent personal information.

3. Device permissions

  • Camera — only when you choose to scan a book page. The photo is sent for text recognition and then discarded.
  • Files / Photos — only the PDF or image you explicitly select is read.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Account and learning data are kept until you delete your account.
  • Photos and text you import or send for narration are not retained by us.
  • Delete your account in the app under Me → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and the learning data stored on our servers. It does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that in your Apple ID settings. Data on your device is removed by deleting the app.
  • If you cannot use the in-app option, email us (see Contact) and we will delete your data.

5. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and each account can access only its own synced data. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. International transfers

Our service providers operate servers in several countries, including the United States. By using the app you understand that your data may be processed outside your country of residence.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under GDPR or California under CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can delete your data in the app as described above, or contact us to exercise any of these rights. We do not sell personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: support@readingchinese.org
