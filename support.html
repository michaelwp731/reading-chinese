Contact us

For questions, bug reports, feedback, or privacy requests, email support@readingchinese.org. Please include your device model and iOS version, and a short description of the problem. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the app, go to Me → Subscription and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Nihao Reading and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also use Me → Subscription → Manage subscription in the app. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

How do I get a refund?
Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

How do I sync my progress to another device?
Go to Me → Account and sign in. Sign in with the same account on your other device and your saved words, bookmarks and reading progress will sync automatically.

How do I delete my account and data?
Go to Me → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and the learning data and reading activity stored on our servers. It does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that in your Apple ID settings first.

How do I turn off personalized recommendations?
Go to Me → Personalized recommendations and switch it off. The app stops recording your reading activity and deletes what was already recorded.

Why did narration switch to my device's voice?
Cloud voice narration has daily or monthly limits, and it needs an internet connection. When it is not available, the app uses your device's built-in voice instead.
