Welcome to Nihao Reading

Learn Chinese by reading stories you actually enjoy — with the tools to understand every word and remember it for good.

Graded stories Read-aloud audio Pinyin & translation Smart flashcards Import your own
1A library that keeps growing

Stories are graded by HSK level, so you always read at the right difficulty.

  • Pick your level

    Every story shows its HSK level. Use the All levels menu on the Stories tab to see only the stories that fit you, and your recommendations will adapt as you read.

  • Browse by topic

    Fables and idioms, myths and legends, history, culture, everyday life, humor, and more. Scroll the home page for a row per topic, or tap View all to see everything in one list.

    MythsIdiomsCultureHistoryLifeHumorWork

  • Always something new

    New stories are added regularly. Use Bookmarked to save the ones you want to read next, and Marked as read to keep track of what you have finished.

2A reader built for learners

Understand every sentence without leaving the page.

  • Lifelike read-aloud

    Tap Listen now to hear the story read naturally, so you learn the rhythm and tones of real Chinese. Adjust the speed from the control bar at the bottom to follow along at your pace.

  • Pinyin when you need it

    Every character comes with pinyin above it. Tap PY in the bottom bar to show or hide it as you get more confident.

  • Tap to translate

    Tap any word for its pinyin, meaning and example sentences. Long-press a word to see the translation of the whole sentence. Prefer the full picture? Tap EN to show the English translation of the story.

3Flashcards that make words stick

Powered by spaced repetition (SRS), the method proven to help you remember more in less time.

  • Save words as you read

    Found a word you want to remember? Tap it, then tap the save button. It goes straight into your Flashcards, along with its meaning and examples.

  • Review at the perfect moment

    Open the Flashcards tab and start a study session. After you reveal a card, tell us how well you knew it. Words you find hard come back soon; words you know well come back later, right before you would forget them.

    Againtoday

  • Build a habit

    Set a daily goal that fits your schedule, and a few minutes a day is all it takes to keep your vocabulary growing.

4Read your own material

Turn anything you want to read into an interactive story, with the same pinyin, translation and read-aloud.

  • Snap a photo

    Open the Import tab and take a photo of a book page, a menu, a sign or a worksheet (or choose one from your photo library). The text is recognized for you, and you can review it before it becomes a story.

  • PDF or pasted text

    Import a PDF, or paste any Chinese text, such as an article, a message from a friend or song lyrics. Everything is organized into pages that are easy to read.

  • Instant translation and read-aloud

    Imported stories work just like the built-in ones: tap a word to look it up, switch on the English translation, and listen to the text read aloud on demand. Your imported books are kept in your library for next time.

💡 A simple way to start today
  1. Open the Stories tab and choose a short story at your level.
  2. Listen while you read, and tap any word you don't know.
  3. Save two or three words, then try a quick Flashcards session.
☁️ Switching phones?

Sign in from the Me tab (it's optional) and your saved words, progress and imported books follow you to your other devices.
